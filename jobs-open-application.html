About Gravity Labs

Gravity Labs is a crypto focused venture studio. We don't write checks and wait. We come up with initial ideas, pressure test those ideas, recruit the founding team, and write the first seed check ourselves. We're owned by defy.vc, which gives us institutional firepower and a network most studios don't have.

Why This Exists

We're a small team, and the roles we post reflect what we know we need today. But the best people we've worked with rarely fit a job description — they show up with proof of work and a point of view, and the role forms around them. If that's you, we want to hear from you even when nothing on our jobs page matches.

What Gets Our Attention

  • Things you've shipped. Products, prototypes, open source, research, writing, communities you've grown. A long list of finished projects tells us more than a polished resume ever will.
  • A point of view. An opinion about where crypto and financial infrastructure go next — and evidence you've acted on it.
  • A specific pitch. Not "I'm a hard worker," but "here's the thing I'd own at Gravity and why you'd move faster with me on it."
  • Speed. Comfort shipping with AI tools, working in ambiguity, and killing your own ideas when the data says so.

Who This Might Be

  • Engineers who prototype fast and don't wait for a spec.
  • Designers who can make a v1 product feel inevitable.
  • Researchers and writers who turn market noise into sharp theses.
  • Operators who've built go-to-market, community, or ops motions from zero.
  • Someone whose background fits no clean category — which is often the point.

Honest expectations: we're not always hiring, and we can't respond to everyone. But we read every application, and we've created roles that didn't exist the day before the right person showed up.
